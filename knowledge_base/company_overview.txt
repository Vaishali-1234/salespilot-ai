# TechNova Solutions — Company Overview

TechNova Solutions is an enterprise technology and digital transformation company founded in 2021 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with regional delivery offices in Pune and Singapore.

## Mission
To empower growing SMBs and mid-market enterprises with production-ready AI automation, resilient cloud infrastructure, custom software engineering, and real-time business intelligence.

## Key Facts
- Customers Served: 180+ B2B clients across FinTech, Healthcare, Retail, SaaS, and Logistics.
- Engineering Team: 95+ certified AI engineers, cloud architects (AWS/GCP/Azure), and full-stack developers.
- Delivery SLA: 99.9% uptime guarantee on managed cloud and AI platforms.
- Security Certifications: ISO 27001 certified, GDPR & India DPDP Act 2023 compliant, SOC 2 Type II controls in place.
- Typical Engagement Model: Fixed-scope sprints, dedicated pods, or annual managed SaaS retainers.
